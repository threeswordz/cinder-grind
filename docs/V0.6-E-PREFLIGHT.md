This reconciliation is not Change Control and introduces no new policy.

## 5. Functional boundary

### 5.1 Project Cash Flow

Stage E may implement a derived Project cash-flow read model from canonical Payment records only.

Required behavior:

- include final `APPROVED`, non-cancelled Payments;
- exclude Draft, Submitted, Rejected and Cancelled Payments;
- group/filter by authorized Project and Payment date;
- represent `INBOUND` as inflow and `OUTBOUND` as outflow;
- count the full Payment amount exactly once;
- do not sum allocations into cash flow;
- do not reduce the Payment cash-flow amount because it is partly/unallocated;
- expose unallocated settlement balance/state separately where useful;
- preserve Payment number, date, direction, counterparty/reference and traceability to source/allocation detail where authorized.
