# Construction ERP — V0.4 Inventory Business Rules

**Status:** APPROVED — Product / Business Owner approval recorded 2026-09-27  
**Decision owner:** Product / Business Owner  
**Entry-gate issue:** #81

These approved rules resolve the Inventory decisions that the architecture baseline intentionally left open and are authoritative for V0.4 implementation.

## Proposed rules

### BR-V04-01 — Inventory numbering

System-assigned identifiers are Company-scoped, immutable, never reused and allocated at creation:

- Goods Receipt: `GRNYYMM-###`, monthly reset
- Material Reservation: `RSVYYMM-###`, monthly reset
- Material Issue: `MIYYMM-###`, monthly reset
- Material Return: `MRTYYMM-###`, monthly reset
- Stock Transfer: `STYYMM-###`, monthly reset

Warehouse codes are user-assigned and unique within the Company.

### BR-V04-02 — Warehouse ownership and lifecycle

A Warehouse belongs to one Company and may be:

- a general Company Warehouse with no fixed Project; or
- a Project/Site Warehouse fixed to one Project.

A Warehouse with Inventory history cannot be deleted or moved to another Company/Project. It may be archived only when it has zero derived on-hand quantity and no active reservation. Historical records remain readable.

### BR-V04-03 — Project stock attribution

Every Stock Transaction retains a nullable Project attribution in addition to Warehouse and Material. Goods Receipt inherits the PO Project. Project/Site Warehouses require matching Project attribution. General Warehouses may hold separately attributed Project stock. WBS, Cost Code and Activity references are optional controlled allocation context and must belong to the same Project.

### BR-V04-04 — Eligible Goods Receipt source

A Goods Receipt line references a material line from the current active approved PO revision. Service lines are not receivable into Inventory. Supplier, Company, Project, Material, UOM and source-chain identity are copied from and validated against the approved PO source.

### BR-V04-05 — Partial, multiple and over-receipt

Partial receipt and multiple Goods Receipts against one PO demand line are allowed.

Cumulative posted quantity is measured across the PO revision lineage using the stable awarded/source demand identity. **V0.4 over-receipt tolerance is zero:** posting is rejected when cumulative non-reversed receipt would exceed the latest approved PO quantity.

Tolerance configuration is deferred through Change Control.

### BR-V04-06 — PO changes after receipt

After a non-reversed Goods Receipt exists:

- a PO cannot be cancelled;
- a new PO revision cannot reduce a source-line quantity below cumulative received quantity;
- receiving continues only against the current active approved revision;
- prior PO and receipt history remains immutable.

A PO cancellation becomes eligible only after all posted receipts have been fully reversed.

### BR-V04-07 — Stock-changing workflow and maker-checker

Goods Receipt, Material Issue, Material Return and Stock Transfer use Draft → Submitted → Approved/Rejected. Final approval and posting occur atomically through the configured Approval Matrix. The creator cannot perform the final approval/post action. Drafts may be edited; submitted, approved, rejected, posted and reversed history is retained.

Material Reservation uses Draft → Active → Fulfilled/Released/Cancelled and requires explicit reservation permission. Reservation does not post physical stock.

### BR-V04-08 — Immutable posting and correction

Posted Inventory documents and Stock Transactions cannot be edited or deleted. Correction uses a full auditable reversal linked to the original document, followed by a new corrected document when required. A reversal records actor, timestamp and reason and posts the exact opposite ledger effect atomically.

### BR-V04-09 — Atomicity and duplicate-effect protection

A posting or reversal commits its source state transition, action history and all ledger rows in one database transaction. Any failure rolls back the complete action. Each post/reversal has a stable idempotency key and database-enforced source uniqueness so client retry or concurrent requests cannot create duplicate stock effects.

### BR-V04-10 — Quantity and UOM

Inventory quantities use the existing controlled decimal precision and must be greater than zero. V0.4 does not perform UOM conversion. A transaction line must use the Material/PO source UOM required by its source context; mismatched UOM is rejected.

### BR-V04-11 — Negative stock

Negative stock is prohibited. Posting is rejected when it would reduce on-hand quantity below zero for the controlled Company, Warehouse, Material and Project attribution. Database transaction locking/serialization must keep concurrent posts from bypassing this rule.

### BR-V04-12 — Ledger and balance ownership

`stock_transactions` is the immutable physical-movement source of truth. Receipt, Issue, Return, Transfer and reversal create ledger rows. Stock Balance is a derived sum of posted, non-duplicated ledger effects and has no editable balance table.

### BR-V04-13 — Reservation and availability

Reservation does not change on-hand stock. Available quantity is:

`derived on-hand quantity - active reserved quantity`

calculated for the same Company, Warehouse, Material and Project attribution. A reservation cannot exceed current available quantity. It has no automatic expiry in V0.4; authorized users explicitly fulfill, release or cancel it with retained history.

### BR-V04-14 — Material Issue

A Material Issue reduces physical stock at posting. It requires Company, Warehouse, Project and valid allocation context. An Issue may consume an active Reservation; reserved quantity belonging to another active Reservation is unavailable. Final posting atomically validates on-hand/available stock and fulfills the linked Reservation quantity.

### BR-V04-15 — Material Return

A Material Return must reference a prior posted Material Issue line. Cumulative non-reversed return quantity cannot exceed the issued quantity. Return restores stock to the specified valid Warehouse and preserves the original Material, UOM, Project and Issue traceability. Returning purchased goods to a Supplier is outside V0.4.

### BR-V04-16 — Stock Transfer

A Stock Transfer posts matched source-negative and destination-positive ledger rows atomically for the same Material, quantity and UOM. Source and destination must differ and belong to the same Company. Inter-company and in-transit workflows are excluded.

Project attribution is preserved by default. An explicit Project-attribution change is allowed only when the actor has access to both source and destination Project contexts and the destination Warehouse permits the target Project; the change is retained in audit/traceability.

### BR-V04-17 — Permissions and audit

Explicit permissions cover Warehouse administration, view, create, submit, approve/post, reverse, reserve, issue, return, transfer, report and Inventory document access. Technical `SYS_ADMIN` provisioning does not silently replace business authority. Every workflow action, post, reversal and material scope change is audited.

### BR-V04-18 — Documents

DOC-008 reuses the existing secure Documents storage/link architecture. Valid targets are Goods Receipt, Material Reservation, Material Issue, Material Return and Stock Transfer. Targets must match Company and Project scope. No second file store is introduced.

### BR-V04-19 — Reporting and costing boundary

Inventory balance and movement reports are read models derived from Warehouse, source documents and Stock Transactions. V0.4 is quantity-based. It does not calculate inventory valuation, Actual Cost, accounting journals, Supplier Invoice matching or payments.

### BR-V04-20 — Test-environment isolation

Full release regression runs against a clean isolated test database. Runtime acceptance against a persistent UAT environment uses unique scenario data and must not invoke destructive fixture cleanup. Both paths remain required before the V0.4 Release Exit Gate.

## Explicit deferrals

The following are deferred and must not be invented during V0.4 implementation:

- configurable over-receipt tolerance
- UOM conversion
- batch/lot/serial/expiry tracking
- quality inspection and quarantine
- inventory valuation/costing
- Supplier return workflow
- inter-company and in-transit transfers
- automated reservation expiry
- reorder planning and demand forecasting
