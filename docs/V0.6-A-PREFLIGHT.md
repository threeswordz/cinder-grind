## Supplier Invoice identity and data contract

- Add Finance-owned `supplier_invoices` and `supplier_invoice_items` persistence consistent with the approved ERD.
- Header requires Company, Project, Supplier, immutable internal Supplier Invoice number, supplier reference, invoice date, optional due date, base-currency total and retained approval/audit state.
- Internal business number uses D06-01: Company-scoped `SIYYMM-###` with MONTHLY reset through the existing Number Sequence infrastructure.
- Supplier reference remains separate and unique per Company + Supplier under the approved baseline.
- One Supplier Invoice belongs to exactly one Project.
- Lines retain line number, description, amount and optional quantity/unit price where supported by the approved ERD, plus optional PO item, Goods Receipt item, same-Project WBS and same-Company Cost Code.
- Header total must equal the retained line total before approval. Server/database integrity must reject mismatches.
- Currency is the owning Company's base currency. No FX conversion or tax/VAT calculation is introduced.

## PO / Goods Receipt traceability contract

- PO/GR linkage is optional; V0.6-A does not require every Supplier Invoice line to be PO-backed.
- A referenced PO item must belong to the same Company, Supplier and Project as the Supplier Invoice.
- A referenced Goods Receipt item must belong to the same Company/Project context and trace to a compatible PO item/Supplier context.
- If both PO item and GR item are supplied, their lineage must be mutually consistent.
- Cross-Company, cross-Supplier and cross-Project source references are rejected at service/database boundaries where practical.
- D06-03 remains explicit: Stage A implements source-reference integrity only. It must not infer matching tolerances, automatic three-way-match approval or quantity/value tolerance policy.

## Lifecycle and approval contract

- Draft Supplier Invoices may edit approved Draft fields/lines only.
- Submission creates/attaches the configured Supplier Invoice Approval Matrix instance and retains submitter/time.
- Final approval requires explicit `finance.supplier_invoice.approve`, current workflow-step authorization, correct state and maker-checker separation.
- Rejection retains decision history.
- Approved/rejected/submitted source history is retained and cannot be physically deleted or silently rewritten through ordinary flows.
- D06-13 remains active: no credit note, debit note, post-approval cancellation or direct approved-invoice mutation is added in V0.6-A.
- Workflow/action retries must not create duplicate state transitions or duplicate audit effects.

## Database integrity and concurrency contract

- All schema changes are forward-only; no already-executed migration may be modified.
- Composite constraints/triggers should enforce same Company/Project/Supplier source integrity where Prisma alone cannot.
- Submitted/approved/rejected identity and decided commercial history are immutable through ordinary paths.
- Physical deletion of retained submitted/terminal Supplier Invoice history is rejected.
- Number allocation and sensitive lifecycle actions follow existing deterministic locking/idempotency patterns.
- Concurrent final approval/edit/source mutation races must serialize or recheck committed state so a stale Draft cannot become approved with invalid totals/source links.
- Any failed state transition, audit write, source-integrity check or approval action rolls back atomically.

## API, UI and permission contract

Use the approved Finance API architecture and explicit permission catalogue.

Stage-A permissions:
- `finance.supplier_invoice.view`
- `finance.supplier_invoice.create`
- `finance.supplier_invoice.edit`
- `finance.supplier_invoice.submit`
- `finance.supplier_invoice.approve`
- `finance.supplier_invoice.reject`

Stage-A routes should support Project-scoped list/create, detail/edit, Draft line maintenance, source selectors, workflow options and submit/approve/reject actions under `/api/v1`.

- Backend authorization is the security boundary.
- Effective Project access is required for list/detail/create/edit/actions unless `projects.access_all` bypasses assignment filtering.
- `projects.access_all` does not bypass D06-17 same-Project integrity and does not grant Finance action/approval permissions.
- Technical SYS_ADMIN receives no implicit Supplier Invoice approval authority.
- FINANCE_OFFICER / FINANCE_MANAGER starter-role behavior must remain consistent with the approved Roles/Permissions baseline.
- The web workspace exposes only the Stage-A Supplier Invoice workflow; Client Invoice, Payment, AP/AR, cash flow and retention surfaces remain out of scope.
