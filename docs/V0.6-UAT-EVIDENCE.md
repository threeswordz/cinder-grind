## Human walkthrough

Use a disposable or approved UAT Company/Project and business-role accounts. Technical SYS_ADMIN must not substitute for a Finance business approver.

### 1. Supplier Invoice → approval → source traceability

1. Open an approved Purchase Order / posted Goods Receipt for the test Project.
2. Create a Supplier Invoice against valid PO/GR lineage.
3. Verify Project, Supplier, source references, invoice amount and retained line detail.
4. Submit through the configured Supplier Invoice workflow.
5. Verify maker-checker separation and approve as the configured checker.
6. Open forward/back trace from PO/GR to the approved Supplier Invoice.

**Expected:** Supplier Invoice remains Project/Supplier/source-consistent, approval evidence is retained, and no tax/FX/GL or matching-tolerance behavior is invented.

### 2. Client Invoice → approval → derived AR

1. Create a Client Invoice for the same authorized Project and Customer.
2. Submit and approve through its configured workflow.
3. Open Accounts Receivable.
4. Reconcile invoice total, allocated receipt amount and outstanding amount.

**Expected:** AR is derived from approved Client Invoice plus valid approved receipt allocations; there is no editable AR balance table.

### 3. Payment → allocation → AP/AR → cancellation history

1. Create an outbound Supplier Payment and allocate some/all of it to an approved Supplier Invoice.
2. Submit and approve through the configured Payment workflow.
3. Verify AP reduces only by the approved allocation amount.
4. Create/approve an inbound Customer Payment and allocation; verify AR reduces by the approved allocation amount.
5. Exercise the approved cancellation path on a test Payment.
6. Verify cancellation retains Payment/approval/allocation history and removes its settlement effect from derived AP/AR.

**Expected:** Payment Project/direction/counterparty identity is retained; maker-checker applies; allocations govern settlement only; cancelled history is retained without continuing settlement effect.

### 4. Subcontract certification → Finance reference / payable retention

1. Open an approved Subcontract Certification with retained withholding.
2. Verify Finance Payment options expose the eligible same-Project Certification.
3. Create a Payment reference/allocation without moving Subcontract source ownership into Finance.
4. Open the payable-retention view.
5. Verify withholding evidence and, where a correction/reversal scenario is used, the linked compensating reversal evidence.

**Expected:** Subcontracts remains source owner, Finance holds settlement/reference evidence, retention history is immutable/source-linked, and no retention release/manual adjustment is available.

### 5. Finance Reports → Project Cash Flow

1. Open **Finance Reports** with a user holding the applicable AP/AR/Payment/Retention view permissions.
2. Select the UAT Project.
3. Reconcile AP, AR, Payment and payable-retention rows against their source workspaces.
4. Create or identify a final approved, non-cancelled INBOUND Payment and confirm its full amount appears as inflow on its Payment date.
5. Create or identify a final approved, non-cancelled OUTBOUND Payment and confirm its full amount appears as outflow.
6. Verify a partly or wholly unallocated approved Payment still contributes its **full Payment amount** to Project Cash Flow while its allocated/unallocated settlement state remains separately visible.
7. Verify allocation rows do not add a second cash-flow amount.
8. Cancel a test Payment and verify it no longer contributes to cash flow while retained Payment history remains readable.
9. Apply a From/To Payment-date filter and verify totals/rows are limited to the inclusive requested range.

**Expected:** Project Cash Flow follows D06-19 / DEC-020 exactly and remains a read-only derived view, not an editable ledger.

### 6. Security and leakage boundaries

1. Use a user with relevant Finance view permission but no assignment to the UAT Project.
2. Verify direct Project cash-flow access is denied.
3. Verify Project selectors/reports do not expose inaccessible Project rows or aggregates.
4. Use technical SYS_ADMIN without Finance Payment authority and verify cash-flow Project/report access is denied.
5. Confirm frontend visibility changes do not bypass backend permission/Project enforcement.

**Expected:** explicit Finance permission and effective Project access are both required. Technical administration authority does not imply Finance business authority.

### 7. Cost/accounting boundary

1. Review Finance Reports and source records.
2. Confirm there is no V0.7 Actual Cost, Paid Cost or Committed Cost ledger created by V0.6-E.
3. Confirm no GL/journal/chart-of-accounts, tax/VAT, FX/multi-currency accounting, retention release/manual adjustment or detailed progress-billing feature has appeared.

**Expected:** approved V0.6 exclusions and COST-014 policy boundary remain intact.
