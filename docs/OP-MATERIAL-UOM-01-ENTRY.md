## Approved quantity and conversion contract

1. **One canonical stock basis.** Each explicitly enabled Company/material has one pinned stock UOM. New controlled stock effects and availability/reservation locks use that basis. Preserve original entered quantity/UOM and immutable normalized quantity/UOM/conversion provenance. PostgreSQL StockTransaction and existing inventory source owners remain canonical; no additional editable stock ledger or balance.
2. **Explicit exact material ratios.** Definitions are positive, versioned, direct source-UOM→stock-UOM ratios scoped to this Company/material. Never infer from unit names, reuse another material/Company's pack size, or introduce conversion chains. A ratio is exact; its mathematical representation must not introduce floating-point or stored-factor rounding.
3. **Explicit effective dates and frozen sources.** Definition revisions have explicit effective calendar dates with deterministic unambiguous selection. Persist the source's conversion business date. Reuse issueDate/transferDate where applicable; require an explicit business date for controlled sources lacking one. Do not silently substitute server-today or a timezone-derived date. Pin the revision/factor when the authorized quantity source is frozen/committed. Source date/factor changes invalidate affected pending decisions; future master data cannot recalculate an already pinned source, approved plan, reservation or posted history.
4. **Exact precision, no silent tolerance.** Preserve UOM precision and existing DECIMAL(18,4) stock bounds with exact arithmetic. Reject a normalized quantity that cannot be represented exactly, exceeds precision/range or uses an absent/ambiguous/inactive mapping before effects. No automatic rounding, truncation, residual balance or invented tolerance. A rounding exception needs a separate concrete Owner decision.
5. **Commercial ownership retained.** Approved PO/RFQ/GR commercial quantity/UOM, cumulative receipt ceilings, price, amount, currency, invoice/payment and financial source rules stay canonical. Stock normalization must not reprice or relabel those facts, enable overreceipt, convert money, or duplicate Actual/Paid Cost. No new Accounting/valuation behavior.
6. **Original-provenance correction.** Returns and reversals reuse the original stored conversion and exact remaining source quantity with current source ownership, availability, locks and approval controls. Same-Company transfers keep approved zero-effect, origin deduction on dispatch and destination credit on actual receipt, explicit receiver assignment and partial/outstanding history. Later factor changes cannot alter the reversal quantity or fabricate receipt.
7. **Counts and legacy activation.** Controlled physical counts state and reconcile the pinned stock UOM. Preserve existing same-UOM behavior, old grants and every historical source/effect row. Do not infer missing old pack sizes, reinterpret old dimensions or bulk-normalize historical balances. Incompatible material activation fails pending a separately reviewed transition. This entry does not approve a migration of retained mixed-unit stock.
8. **Requests, plans and reports.** Use the same base dimension and pinned provenance for material-linked demand, approved plan quantities, reservations, fulfillment and reports while retaining entered values/labels. Preserve existing plan, overage, request, signed-evidence and approval limits. A later larger pack must not bypass an approved base-quantity limit. Do not invent BOQ work-to-material recipes, alter work progress, or automatically convert subcontract measured-work units/commercial amounts.

**Synthetic example:** receive2cartons at20pieces/carton=40pieces; issue1carton leaves20. A future24-piece revision leaves that balance unchanged; reversal of the original issue restores20, not24; a new receipt under24 adds24. Existing source limits/correction and availability guards still apply. A ratio3source-units:1stock-unit can be exact as a definition, while an entered quantity whose result is nonrepresentable must still fail. These are disposable proof facts, not real conversion values or tolerances.
