## Product / Business Owner walkthrough

Use an authorized Project Manager, a distinct configured approver, and an unassigned Project user. Preserve transaction numbers and the scenario suffix as evidence.

1. Create a Project/Site Warehouse and verify an unassigned Project user cannot list or read its stock.
2. From an approved material PO, post two partial Goods Receipts. Confirm combined quantity respects the approved PO line ceiling and appears in derived Stock Balance.
3. Reserve available stock against the Project/WBS/Activity; confirm physical on-hand is unchanged and available stock decreases. Issue linked stock with maker-checker approval; confirm one negative ledger effect and Reservation fulfillment.
4. Return part of that posted Issue; confirm the remaining returnable ceiling and positive ledger effect. Reverse the Return before the Issue and verify exact balance restoration with retained history.
5. Transfer stock between two different authorized Warehouses. Confirm a separate approver posts matched OUT/IN effects, source availability cannot fall below Active Reservations, and retries do not duplicate effects. Reverse the Transfer and confirm exact opposite effects.
6. Attach and retrieve Inventory transaction evidence through the existing Documents area. Verify target options for Goods Receipt, Reservation, Issue, Return and Transfer, plus denial for an unassigned Project.
7. Filter the balance and movement reports by Project, Warehouse, Material, type, source and date. Verify exact quantities, source numbers, reversal history and no records from an inaccessible Project.
8. Confirm no V0.4 transaction creates Finance, Actual Cost or valuation postings.
