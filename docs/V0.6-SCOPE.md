## 1. Purpose

V0.6 connects approved Procurement, Inventory, Subcontracts and Project/Customer sources to Finance-owned invoice, receivable/payable, payment, settlement and cash-flow records without creating a second source of truth.

Finance owns the canonical Supplier Invoice, Client Invoice, Payment and Payment Allocation records. AP/AR and Project Cash Flow are derived views. Cost Control remains V0.7.

## 2. Approved requirement boundary

### In scope

- **PROC-020** — Procure-to-Pay Traceability
- **SUB-011** — Finance Payment Reference
- **FIN-001–FIN-010** — Supplier/Client Invoices, AP/AR, Payments, Allocations, Retention Accounting, Subcontract payment references, Project Cash Flow
- **FIN-012–FIN-014** — Supplier Invoice, Client Invoice and Payment approval
- **COST-014** — cost-recognition policy documentation only; no V0.7 Cost Control implementation
- **DOC-009** — Subcontract and Finance document associations
- **RPT-008** — basic Finance reporting

### Related but not implemented as V0.6 Cost Control

- **FIN-011** remains targeted to V0.7. V0.6 creates the canonical approved payment/allocation evidence that V0.7 may later consume as Paid Cost.
- V0.6 must not create a separate Paid Cost, Actual Cost or Committed Cost ledger.

## 3. Approved functional scope

### 3.1 Supplier Invoices

- Finance-owned Supplier Invoice header and line items.
- Company + Supplier identity, supplier reference, invoice/due dates, base-currency amounts and approval lifecycle.
- Optional line linkage to approved Purchase Order items and posted/non-reversed Goods Receipt items where applicable.
- Project is required at line level; optional same-Project WBS and same-Company Cost Code.
- **Pending D06-17 owner decision:** recommended V0.6 rule is **one Project per Supplier Invoice**: every line and any linked PO/GR source must belong to that same Project, and Supplier Payment settlement/cash-flow attribution inherits that Project. A supplier invoice spanning multiple Projects would be unsupported in the initial V0.6 baseline unless the owner instead approves a line-level allocation/proration and Project-visibility design.
- Procure-to-Pay traceability remains navigable from approved PO/GRN through Supplier Invoice and Payment.
- Supplier invoice approval reuses the configurable Approval Matrix and maker-checker controls.

### 3.2 Client Invoices

- Finance-owned Client Invoice header and line items.
- One Project and valid same-Company Customer.
- Generic line descriptions and amounts.
- Configurable approval workflow and maker-checker.
- Detailed progress-billing methodology is not introduced without explicit Change Control.

### 3.3 AP / AR

- Outstanding Supplier and Client balances are derived from approved invoices minus valid approved/cancel-safe allocations.
- No manually editable AP/AR balance table or second financial ledger.

### 3.4 Payments and allocations

- Finance-owned inbound/outbound Payment records.
- OUTBOUND payments settle Supplier Invoices and/or certified Subcontract claims.
- INBOUND payments settle Client Invoices.
- Payment approval uses the existing Approval Matrix and maker-checker.
- Allocation rows are canonical settlement evidence and remain historically traceable.
- Duplicate-effect protection and transaction atomicity are mandatory.
- Cumulative allocation may not exceed the approved Payment amount or the target outstanding balance.
- Counterparty, Company, direction, Project visibility and base-currency compatibility are enforced.

### 3.5 Subcontract Finance hand-off and retention

- Finance can allocate approved outbound Payments to V0.5 certified Subcontract claims/certifications.
- **Approved D06-14:** the ordinary payment-allocation ceiling for a certification is its V0.5 `net_certified_amount`, less prior active allocations; withheld retention is not payable through this path.
- **Approved D06-15:** if the certification currency differs from Company base currency, Finance handoff is rejected/deferred with no conversion or numeric reinterpretation.
- **Approved D06-16:** certification reversal is blocked while active Finance Payment allocations remain; after Finance cancellations clear those allocations, reversal creates a linked compensating retention-withholding correction where applicable, not a retention release/payout.
- Subcontracts displays Finance-owned payment status/reference without duplicating payment data.
- Finance retention accounting consumes V0.5 certified withholding evidence.
- Proposed V0.6 retention scope is **payable retention withholding/balance visibility only**.
- Retention release and user-initiated adjustment transaction policy remains excluded unless the Product / Business Owner explicitly approves it.

### 3.6 Project Cash Flow and Finance reporting

- Project cash-flow visibility is derived from approved Finance source transactions and project-attributable allocations.
- An unallocated Payment amount is not attributed to a Project until an approved allocation/source relationship provides Project context; it may remain visible only in authorized Company-level Finance views.
- Basic AP, AR, Payment, retention and Project cash-flow reporting is permission- and Project-scope-aware.
- Reports do not create or mutate source transactions.

### 3.7 Documents

DOC-009 is **approved under D06-09 / DEC-017** to extend the existing Documents abstraction and authorization model to the following Finance/Subcontract targets. No second file store or Finance-specific document silo is created.

Approved targets:
- Supplier Invoice
- Client Invoice
- Payment
- Subcontract Agreement
- Subcontract Work Order
- Subcontract Claim
- Subcontract Certification
- Subcontract Variation

This eight-target list is final for the approved V0.6 baseline; any expansion requires Change Control.

## 4. Explicit exclusions

Unless explicitly approved through the entry gate / Change Control, V0.6 excludes:

- tax/VAT calculation, tax-code engine or statutory tax reporting;
- FX conversion, foreign-exchange gains/losses or multi-currency accounting;
- accounting journals, general ledger, chart of accounts, accrual engine or statutory accounting postings;
- amount-based approval thresholds;
- supplier invoice matching tolerances / auto-match policy;
- detailed Client progress-billing methodology;
- Supplier/Client credit notes, debit notes and post-approval invoice cancellation unless separately approved through Change Control;
- retention release/adjustment authorization;
- V0.7 Cost Control read models/ledgers, direct-cost posting, forecast/variance/profit calculations;
- V0.8 management dashboards/analytics;
- mandatory paid services, commercial reporting libraries or paid runtime dependencies.

## 5. Cost-state boundary

The release must preserve:

`Committed Cost != Actual Cost != Paid Cost`

even when amounts happen to be numerically equal.

- Procurement/Subcontracts remain sources of commitment evidence.
- Finance invoices/certifications may become Actual Cost only according to the separately approved D06-08 / COST-014 policy.
- Approved Finance payments/allocations are settlement evidence and future V0.7 Paid Cost sources.
- V0.6 itself does not create a Cost Control ledger.

## 6. Approved data-model baseline

Reuse the logical ERD Finance entities:

- `supplier_invoices`
- `supplier_invoice_items`
- `client_invoices`
- `client_invoice_items`
- `payments`
- `supplier_payment_allocations`
- `client_receipt_allocations`
- `subcontract_payment_allocations`
- `retention_ledger_entries`

Baseline rules:

- UUID internal identity; configurable immutable business numbers.
- Company ownership and composite integrity where references cross modules.
- DECIMAL commercial values; no JavaScript floating-point authority for money.
- Company base currency for prototype Finance; no FX conversion.
- Approved/submitted financial history is not physically deleted or directly rewritten.
- Supplier/Client Invoice totals must equal the sum of retained line amounts under the approved Draft/approval lifecycle.
- AP/AR and cash flow are derived, not independently editable balances.
- Forward-only migrations only; already-executed migrations are never modified.

## 7. Proposed API baseline

Extend the existing Finance resource patterns under `/api/v1`:

- Supplier Invoice list/detail/create/edit/submit/approve/reject
- Client Invoice list/detail/create/edit/submit/approve/reject
- Payment list/detail/create/edit/submit/approve/reject/cancel
- Payment allocation create/remove while Payment remains in an allowed pre-approval state, plus retained approved allocation history
- AP/AR derived views
- Project cash-flow derived view
- Finance retention view
- Finance/Subcontract document-target routes through the existing Documents module

Exact route names and mutation-state rules must follow approved BR-V06 rules and are not implementation authorization.

## 8. Authorization baseline

Reuse existing explicit permissions in `docs/ROLES-PERMISSIONS.md`:

- `finance.supplier_invoice.*`
- `finance.client_invoice.*`
- `finance.payment.*`
- `finance.ap.view`
- `finance.ar.view`
- `finance.cashflow.view`
- `finance.retention.view`

Rules:

- `projects.access_all` bypasses Project assignment only; it grants no Finance action authority.
- Technical `SYS_ADMIN` does not receive implicit business approval authority.
- Project-scoped references and reports enforce backend Project access.
- Company isolation is always enforced.
- Approval requires explicit permission + configured Approval Matrix step + maker-checker + valid scope/state.

## 9. Audit / integrity / concurrency baseline

- Material lifecycle actions are audited with actor/time/action/context.
- Approved transaction history is retained and ordinary application flows do not hard-delete it.
- Sensitive create/payment actions use durable duplicate-effect protection.
- Payment + allocation writes are atomic.
- Concurrent allocations serialize/recheck outstanding balances so two requests cannot over-allocate a Payment or target.
- Approval/cancellation races recheck committed winner state.
- Cross-Company, cross-Project and invalid source references are rejected at service and database boundaries where practical.
- No second AP/AR/Payment ledger may be added by convenience. The ERD-defined `retention_ledger_entries` is the only explicit Finance retention ledger proposed for V0.6 and must retain source links rather than replace V0.5 certification evidence.

## 10. Test / UAT baseline

Automated coverage must include:

- clean migration-from-zero and migration status;
- Supplier Invoice lifecycle, source integrity, approval, unauthorized access and maker-checker;
- Client Invoice lifecycle, Project/Customer integrity, approval and AR;
- inbound/outbound Payment lifecycle and approval;
- supplier/client/subcontract allocation ceilings and idempotent retries;
- concurrent allocation, approval and cancellation races;
- AP/AR and cash-flow derivation;
- retention withholding/balance visibility;
- SUB-011 payment-reference visibility;
- DOC-009 target authorization;
- Company/Project isolation and direct API bypass denial;
- audit/history immutability;
- prior-release regression and live HTTP acceptance.

Human V0.6 UAT remains a separate mandatory release-exit gate and cannot be self-approved by automation.

- No new runtime dependency is required by this approved baseline.
