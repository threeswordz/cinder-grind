## Agreement retention configuration contract

- Add `retentionRate DECIMAL(5,2)` constrained to 0.00–100.00 and optional nonnegative `retentionCap DECIMAL(18,2)` to the Agreement commercial snapshot.
- Add the same retention fields to the original Agreement Version snapshot so first approval retains the approved commercial terms. Administrative revisions copy them unchanged and cannot edit them under BR-V05-06.
- Existing pre-Stage-D Agreements are forward-migrated to `retentionRate = 0.00`, `retentionCap = NULL`. This is a compatibility encoding of the historical behavior where no withholding configuration/effect existed; it does not retroactively impose retention.
- New Agreement Draft creation exposes retention rate/cap with system defaults 0.00/null. Drafts may edit them before first submission. First submission/approval freezes them as commercial terms.
- No approved Agreement action changes retention rate/cap after first approval. A request to amend approved retention terms is outside BR-V05-06/16 and stops for Change Control.

## Certification data and lifecycle contract

- Add retained `SubcontractCertification` linked to one Company/Project/Agreement, one assessed Claim and its retained Assessment.
- Identity is immutable Company/month `SCTYYMM-###`; currency is inherited from Agreement.
- Draft stores tax-exclusive nonnegative `certifiedGross` plus creator/time. Draft amount is editable only before submission.
- Lifecycle is `DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED/REJECTED`; an `APPROVED` certification may move only to `REVERSED` through the explicit correction action.
- A Claim may have at most one active certification (`DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `APPROVED`). Rejected/reversed certification rows remain immutable history and do not prevent a later eligible correction path.
- Submission starts configured Approval Matrix workflow and records workflow/action evidence. Final approval records immutable final approver/time and freezes the source Assessment, gross amount and retention snapshot.
- Final approval requires the Claim still be `ASSESSED`, its Assessment still be accepted, the Agreement still be approved/non-cancelled, gross in [0, assessed amount], and cumulative non-reversed approved certification not above the current Agreement ceiling.
- Certification rejection retains certification identity, gross source amount, Approval history and decision. A later Draft certification for the same still-assessed Claim may be created after rejection; rejected rows never consume certified/retention totals.
- Approved Certification correction is reversal only. Reversal requires explicit permission, reason, actor/time and stable retry evidence; the original approved certification remains immutable apart from reversal evidence.

## Approved certification correction integration

- Reversal removes only the certification's derived commercial/withholding effect from non-reversed cumulative totals. It is not a cash payment, refund or retention release.
- After reversal, the original Claim remains immutable `ASSESSED` while correction is prepared; it cannot receive another certification.
- A reversed-certification Claim becomes eligible for a linked replacement Draft. On successful replacement submission, the predecessor atomically changes `ASSESSED → REPLACED`, stops consuming active Claim ceiling, and retains its accepted Assessment plus reversed Certification history.
- Forward-only Stage-D database guards extend Stage-C replacement consistency narrowly: `ASSESSED → REPLACED` is valid only with retained `REVERSED` certification evidence and a submitted linked replacement. Existing rejected/withdrawn correction rules remain unchanged.
- An abandoned replacement Draft does not prematurely remove the predecessor from the active Claim ceiling, preserving Stage-C correction safety.

## Retention withholding calculation contract

- On final certification approval, snapshot Agreement `retentionRate` and `retentionCap`.
- Calculate raw withholding from `certifiedGross × retentionRate / 100` using decimal arithmetic and half-up rounding to two decimal places.
- With no cap, `retainedAmount` is the rounded product.
- With a cap, compute remaining cap as approved cap minus cumulative retained amount from other non-reversed approved certifications under the Agreement, floored at zero; retained amount is the lesser of rounded product and remaining cap.
- `retainedAmount` is nonnegative and cannot exceed `certifiedGross`. `netCertifiedAmount = certifiedGross - retainedAmount`.
- A 0.00% rate yields zero withholding. A zero cap also yields zero withholding.
- Reversed certifications are excluded from cumulative certified and retained totals while their original snapshots/results remain immutable history.
- Stage D creates no retention-release transaction, Finance posting, payment status or second ledger.

## Concurrency and integrity baseline

1. Certification submit/final approval/rejection/reversal and Agreement cancellation share deterministic Agreement-row locking with Claim/Assessment state rechecks.
2. Concurrent certification approvals cannot exceed Agreement ceiling or retention cap; final approval calculates withholding only after Agreement lock and current cumulative totals are read.
3. Approval Matrix multi-step action remains maker-checker and role-gated; only final approval freezes certification and withholding snapshot.
4. Cancellation versus certification final approval has one valid winner; the loser rechecks Agreement state after lock.
5. Certification reversal versus competing approval/cancellation/retry cannot create duplicate reversal or double-release any commercial/retention effect.
6. Linked replacement submission after reversal serializes with Agreement/Claim state and cannot mark the predecessor REPLACED without the reversed certification evidence.
7. Stable retry keys never bypass current Project visibility, maker-checker or current lifecycle state.
8. No certification/retention action relies on UI visibility, operational-status labels or technical SYS_ADMIN for business authority.
