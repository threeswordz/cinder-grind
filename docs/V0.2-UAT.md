## 5. Known V0.2 boundaries

- Frappe Gantt is visualization-only and replaceable.
- Site Material usage is observational and does not post Inventory.
- Site weather is a local observation; no external weather service is required.
- inspections are lightweight references, not a full QA/QC workflow.
- Equipment Maintenance remains Future.
- Equipment cost allocation remains V0.7.
- Project Type configuration is the deferred remainder of Should-Have ADM-008; Activity Types are delivered.
- V0.3 Procurement/BOQ/Budget and later-release functionality is excluded.
