## Register, identity and authority

### BR-V05-01 — Subcontractor ownership
Maintain a Company-owned `subcontractors` register as the Subcontracts module's source of truth under SUB-001. Code is Company-unique, active/inactive state is retained, and historical references are never deleted. Register maintenance requires an explicit Company permission, not Project membership.

### BR-V05-02 — Supplier linkage
An optional nullable same-Company Supplier link may assist lookup/deduplication; Subcontractor identity and ownership remain independent. Replacing the owned register with Supplier is a SUB-001 Requirements change and needs separate recorded Change Control.

### BR-V05-03 — Numbering
Proposed system numbers allocated once at creation, Company-scoped and never reused: agreement `SCYYMM-###`, claim `SCLYYMM-###`, certification `SCTYYMM-###`, variation `SVOYYMM-###`, monthly reset; Work Order `WO-###` sequential and unique within its agreement. Rejected/withdrawn identifiers remain in history. These formats/reset choices require explicit approval.

### BR-V05-04 — Project scope and business authority
Agreements and downstream Work Orders, Claims, assessments, certifications, variations and reports require explicit permission and database-derived Project access. `projects.access_all` bypasses assignment filtering only. Technical SYS_ADMIN grants no business approval. Same-Company/Project references are enforced at API and database boundaries.

## Agreement and Work Order

### BR-V05-05 — Agreement lifecycle and status
System approval state is `DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED/REJECTED`; configured operational status is separate. Final approval uses the existing configurable Approval Matrix and maker-checker. An approved agreement may be marked cancelled with audited reason only when there is no pending submitted Work Order or Variation, active approved Work Order allocation, active submitted/assessed Claim or non-reversed certification. Final approval of every downstream Work Order, Variation, assessment and certification rechecks that the agreement is approved and not cancelled under the same transaction/lock; a cancel-then-approve sequence must fail. Inactive Subcontractors cannot be used for new agreements.

### BR-V05-06 — Original agreement and revisions
The first approved original agreement value and Scope of Work are immutable history. Agreement revisions may change only non-commercial administrative fields, with versioned Draft and fresh approval; prior versions remain readable. All subsequent commercial value or Scope of Work changes use BR-V05-16 approved Variation Orders exclusively. Current authorized ceiling is original approved value plus non-reversed approved Variation deltas; no revision can change that amount or fold a Variation into the base. No approved row is silently edited/deleted.

### BR-V05-07 — Work Order allocation and commitment
A Work Order references one approved, non-cancelled agreement, has its own scope and positive amount, and uses Draft → Submitted → Approved/Rejected via configured maker-checker approval. Final approval rechecks the agreement is not cancelled. Sum of active approved Work Order amounts cannot exceed current approved agreement value plus approved signed Variations. The **agreement plus Variations** is one commercial commitment ceiling; Work Orders allocate within it and are shown separately, never added on top as a second commitment.

### BR-V05-08 — Scope and work dimensions
Agreement Scope of Work is required text; Work Order scope is required text for its portion. Optional WBS must belong to the agreement Project; optional Cost Code must belong to the same Company and remains independent of WBS. No separate Task entity is created.

## Progress Claim and assessment

### BR-V05-09 — Claim period and lines
A Claim references one approved, non-cancelled agreement and an inclusive start/end period with start <= end. One active Claim may exist for the exact agreement/period pair; rejected/withdrawn/replaced history remains. Lines carry positive monetary values and may reference a valid approved Work Order in the same agreement. If the agreement has any active approved Work Orders, every Claim line references one of them; otherwise the line references agreement scope. No automatic percentage-complete or measured-quantity methodology is introduced.

### BR-V05-10 — Cumulative claim ceiling
Claim amounts are **period increments**, not a second cumulative balance field. Sum of active submitted/assessed/certified Claim amounts (excluding rejected/withdrawn/replaced Claims) must not exceed the current approved agreement-plus-Variation ceiling. When a line references a Work Order, its active cumulative claimed amount must also stay within that approved Work Order amount. Concurrent submissions serialize both checks. Claim, assessed and certified values remain distinct.

### BR-V05-11 — Claim correction/history
Draft is editable; submission freezes source lines. An authorized user may withdraw a submitted Claim before assessment with reason; a replacement Claim links to the prior Claim and retains history. An assessed Claim is corrected by rejecting/voiding its assessment through an auditable action, then submitting a replacement Claim; an approved certification requires BR-V05-13's correction path first. No physical deletion of submitted history.

### BR-V05-12 — Assessment
An explicitly permitted assessor records a nonnegative assessed amount not exceeding the submitted Claim amount, with reason/actor/time and source lines retained. Assessment does not rewrite claimed value. An assessment can be rejected before certification; corrections retain earlier decision history. Assessment is not a Finance posting or an approved certification.

## Certification and retention

### BR-V05-13 — Payment Certification and correction
One certification belongs to one assessed Claim under an approved, non-cancelled agreement. A configured Approval Matrix final approver distinct from the maker approves certified gross amount in [0, assessed amount]; cumulative non-reversed certified amount cannot exceed the current agreement ceiling. Approval freezes certification, actor/time and source assessment. To correct an erroneous approved certification in V0.5, an authorized reversal with reason retains the original and reverses its derived commercial/withholding effect, then the original Claim is marked replaced and a new linked Claim follows the normal assessment/certification route. The replaced Claim no longer consumes the active claim ceiling; its history remains. Reversal is not a cash payment or retention release.

### BR-V05-14 — Retention withholding calculation
The approved agreement stores a withholding rate between 0% and 100% and optional nonnegative aggregate cap; a null cap means no cap. For each approved certification, withheld amount is `min(round(certified_gross × rate / 100, 2), remaining_cap)` when capped, or the rounded product when uncapped. A rate of 0 yields zero. The approved rate/cap and calculated result are snapshotted on certification. Net certified equals gross less withheld. Decimal rounding is half-up to two decimal places; no currency conversion occurs.

### BR-V05-15 — Retention boundary
V0.5 records withheld retention only as Subcontracts commercial history and reports. It does not authorize release, payout, Finance retention accounting, cash settlement or payment status. Certification reversal corrects the historical commercial effect with retained traceability, not a retention release transaction.

## Variations, reporting and currency

### BR-V05-16 — Variation Order effect
A Variation is Draft → Submitted → Approved/Rejected through the configured Approval Matrix and maker-checker. It records signed scope/value change and reason against one approved, non-cancelled agreement. Final approval rechecks the agreement is not cancelled. Approved Variations are the sole path for changing commercial value or Scope of Work after original agreement approval; only their non-reversed deltas affect the current authorized ceiling. A reducing approval is rejected if the resulting ceiling falls below active approved Work Order allocation, active submitted/assessed claimed value or non-reversed certified value. Original value and earlier approval history remain unchanged.

### BR-V05-17 — Operational status and cancellation
Agreement operational status uses Company-configured approved values; system approval and cancellation states are distinct. Archiving a Subcontractor or closing an agreement never removes transactions. Cancel/reverse operations with downstream effects require the explicit guards in BR-V05-05/13/16, a reason, actor and audit event. No status label by itself grants approval authority.

### BR-V05-18 — Derived reporting
Project-scoped, read-only reporting derives original agreement, approved Variation delta, current ceiling, approved Work Order allocation, active claimed value, assessed value, non-reversed certified gross, withheld retention and net certification from source records. Filters do not bypass Project scope. Commercial commitment is never labeled Actual Cost or Paid Cost; V0.5 creates no second financial ledger. Later V0.7 may consume approved source values under its own policy.

### BR-V05-19 — Amount denomination and deferred scope
One explicit currency code is recorded on each agreement and inherited unchanged by its Work Orders, Claims, certifications and Variations. All V0.5 amounts are entered **excluding tax**; V0.5 calculates no tax/VAT or FX and rejects mismatched currency references. This commercial amount basis was approved on 2026-09-29. SUB-011 Finance Payment Reference and DOC-009 transaction document targets remain V0.6; existing generic Project Documents remain under their current scope. No paid mandatory runtime dependency is introduced (DEC-008).

### BR-V05-20 — Atomicity, tests and human gate
Approval, cancellation/reversal and value-ceiling checks commit atomically with audit/history. Stable action keys and database uniqueness protect retries; deterministic locking protects competing agreement, Work Order, Claim, certification and Variation actions. Full regression uses a clean isolated database; persistent UAT uses unique scenario data without destructive fixture cleanup. Product / Business Owner UAT and explicit release acceptance remain separate from automated tests.
