## Claim data and lifecycle contract

- `SubcontractClaim` belongs to one Company, Project and approved non-cancelled Agreement and carries immutable `SCLYYMM-###` identity, inclusive period start/end, inherited currency, lifecycle state, optional replacement source link, creator/submission/withdrawal/replacement evidence and retained timestamps.
- Claim lifecycle implementation maps approved behavior to `DRAFT → SUBMITTED`; terminal/correction states are `WITHDRAWN`, `REJECTED` and `REPLACED`; `ASSESSED` represents a Claim with the current accepted assessment. These states implement BR-V05-09–12 only and do not introduce certification.
- One active Claim exists per exact Agreement + period pair. Active means a non-terminal Claim (`DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `ASSESSED`). Rejected/withdrawn/replaced history remains retained and permits a linked replacement.
- Draft period and lines are editable. Submission freezes period/source lines and records submitter/time.
- Each line stores a positive tax-exclusive DECIMAL(18,2) amount and optional approved Work Order reference. Claim total is source-derived from retained lines rather than separately editable.
- If any active approved Work Order exists for the Agreement at submission, every line requires one and each referenced Work Order must be approved, same Company/Project/Agreement. Otherwise lines are Agreement-scope lines with null Work Order.
- Submission locks the Agreement, rechecks Agreement approval/cancellation, exact-period uniqueness, Work Order references and both cumulative ceilings before changing state.
- Claim amounts are period increments. Cumulative active submitted/assessed Claim totals cannot exceed the current approved Agreement ceiling. Cumulative active amount per Work Order cannot exceed that approved Work Order amount.
- Draft Claims do not consume commercial ceiling until submitted. Rejected/withdrawn/replaced Claims do not consume active ceiling.
- Concurrent submissions for the same Agreement serialize on the Agreement row so neither Agreement ceiling nor Work Order ceiling can be bypassed.
- Withdrawal is allowed only while SUBMITTED and before any accepted assessment; it requires explicit permission, reason, actor/time and stable retry evidence.
- Replacement Claim requires a terminal rejected/withdrawn predecessor under the same Agreement. The new Claim links the predecessor; the predecessor is retained and marked/reported as replaced only when the replacement is successfully submitted, avoiding loss of history from abandoned replacement Drafts.
- Submitted/assessed/rejected/withdrawn/replaced Claims and submitted source lines cannot be physically deleted. Submitted source period/lines cannot be edited.

## Assessment contract

- Assessment is distinct from claimed value. It never rewrites Claim lines or Claim total.
- `SubcontractClaimAssessment` is retained history linked to one submitted Claim and stores nonnegative tax-exclusive DECIMAL(18,2) assessed amount, required reason, assessor actor/time, decision state and rejection evidence when applicable.
- An explicitly permitted assessor may record an assessment only for a SUBMITTED Claim under an approved non-cancelled Agreement.
- Assessed amount may be zero but cannot exceed the submitted Claim total.
- Final assessment recording locks/rechecks the Agreement and Claim in one transaction, records audit/history and moves the Claim to `ASSESSED`. No Finance posting, certification, retention effect or cost recognition occurs.
- An accepted assessment may be rejected before certification by an explicitly permitted action with required reason. Rejection retains the assessment and its original amount/actor/time, records rejector/time/reason, marks the Claim `REJECTED`, and enables a linked replacement Claim.
- Stage C supports no in-place assessed-value edit. Correction is rejection plus replacement Claim as approved by BR-V05-11/12.
- Certification existence is deferred to Stage D; the Stage-C rejection guard is structured so Stage D can later deny rejection once an active certification exists without rewriting Stage-C history.

## Concurrency and integrity baseline

1. Claim submission, Claim withdrawal, Assessment finalization/rejection and Agreement cancellation share deterministic Agreement-row locking.
2. Concurrent Claim submissions cannot exceed the Agreement ceiling or any Work Order amount.
3. Concurrent exact-period submissions have one valid winner; the loser fails without partial audit/replay effects.
4. Cancellation versus Claim submission/Assessment finalization has one valid winner; the losing action rechecks Agreement state after lock.
5. Assessment finalization rechecks submitted Claim state, current total and approved/non-cancelled Agreement after locks are held.
6. Stable retry keys are actor/action/entity/payload bound and never bypass current Project visibility.
7. No Claim/Assessment action relies on UI visibility, configured operational-status labels or technical SYS_ADMIN role codes for business authority.
