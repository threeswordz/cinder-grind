## Variation identity and data contract

- Add retained `SubcontractVariation` linked immutably to one Company, Project and approved Agreement.
- Numbering follows BR-V05-03: Company/month scoped `SVOYYMM-###`, allocated once and never reused. Rejected/reversed identifiers remain history.
- Currency is inherited from the Agreement; Stage E accepts no independent currency, tax/VAT or FX input.
- Draft stores a signed DECIMAL(18,2) commercial value delta, scope-change description, business reason, creator/time and stable create/retry identity.
- The signed value delta may express an increase, reduction or zero-value scope-only change; the approved baseline does not require a separate effective-date or valuation policy. Stage E therefore introduces no effective-date field and no retroactive claim-eligibility rule.
- Original Agreement value/scope never changes. Reported current ceiling is always derived from the immutable original approved value plus approved non-reversed Variation deltas.

## Variation lifecycle and commercial effect

- Lifecycle is `DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED/REJECTED`; an `APPROVED` Variation may move only to `REVERSED` through the explicit audited reversal path authorized by BR-V05-16/17/20.
- Submission starts configured Approval Matrix workflow under entity type `SUBCONTRACT_VARIATION`; final approval requires maker-checker and explicit Variation approval permission.
- Final approval and reversal recheck the Agreement is approved and not cancelled while holding the deterministic Agreement lock.
- Commercial effect begins only when approval commits. There is no separate effective-date scheduler or claim-eligibility recalculation policy in V0.5.
- Approval of a reducing delta is rejected when the resulting current ceiling would fall below any protected amount: active approved Work Order allocation, active submitted/assessed Claim value, or non-reversed certified value.
- Reversal removes only that Variation's derived ceiling effect while retaining the immutable approved row and reversal actor/time/reason. Reversing a positive Variation is itself a reducing ceiling effect and must satisfy the same protected-amount guard before commit.
- Rejected Variations have no ceiling effect. Reversed Variations remain readable history and are excluded from current approved Variation delta.
- Approval/rejection/reversal action keys are actor/action/entity/material-payload bound; retries never bypass current permission, Project visibility, maker-checker or lifecycle checks.

## Database integrity and concurrency contract

- Forward-only migrations add Variation persistence, approval/action/history references and explicit Variation permissions. No executed migration is modified.
- Composite references enforce same Company/Project/Agreement identity. Database guards reject cross-Company/Project linkage, mutable submitted/approved identity, invalid lifecycle evidence and physical deletion of retained submitted/terminal history.
- Approval/cancellation/reversal/ceiling checks serialize on the Agreement in the same deterministic lock discipline already used by downstream Subcontracts actions.
- Concurrent positive/reducing approvals, Agreement cancellation and Variation reversal must converge to one valid serial outcome; a waiter rechecks committed ceiling/state after lock acquisition.
- Agreement cancellation continues to reject pending submitted Variation evidence as required by BR-V05-05 and cannot race a Variation final approval.
- Any failed workflow action, audit write, state change or ceiling validation rolls back the entire transaction.

## Derived reporting contract

- Reporting is Project-scoped, read-only and derived at query time from canonical Subcontracts source records.
- Per Agreement, expose original approved value, approved non-reversed Variation delta, current authorized ceiling, active approved Work Order allocation, active claimed value, assessed value, non-reversed certified gross, withheld retention and net certification.
- Claimed, assessed, certified, withheld and net measures remain distinct. Agreement plus approved Variations is commercial commitment; Work Orders are allocation within that commitment and are never added again as a second commitment.
- Filters are applied only after backend Company/Project authorization and may not reveal unauthorized Projects through rows, counts, aggregates or selector options.
- Rejected/reversed history is available only through authorized history/detail views and affects current totals exactly as the approved BRs specify.
- Reports never label commercial commitment as Actual Cost or Paid Cost, never infer cash payment status and create no second financial/reporting ledger.
