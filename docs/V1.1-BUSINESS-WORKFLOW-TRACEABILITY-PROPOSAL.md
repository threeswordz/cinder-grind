## Critical invariants from approved GitHub baselines

1. **No new V1.1 business policy without owner-approved Change Control.** The PDF's role assignments are candidates, not authorization to bypass permission/approval configuration.
2. Budget baseline: `BUD-003..006` retain the first approved Original Budget and immutable approved revision history. Never permit direct mutation of already approved history. Pre-construction working edit/reapproval is an **owner decision**, not an implementation shortcut.
3. `PROC-020` and V0.6 Finance are canonical procure-to-pay sources. A My Work aggregate must link to owners' records; **never create a second PR/PO/AP/Payment ledger**.
4. `INV-004/011`: stock transfers post linked source/destination ledger effects. Adding a receiving acknowledgment must not double-post quantity/cost.
5. V0.5 `SUB-009` approves VO-based agreement scope/value changes. The PDF legacy **new SC for extra work** statement (#21) must be reconciled through a documented owner choice before any behavioral change.
6. V0.6 `D06-17` **one Project per Supplier Invoice**, `D06-18` **one Project per Payment**, and Company/base-currency scope remain strict. No generic other-receipt, internal bank transfer or payroll payment should be shoehorned into these APIs.
7. Roles are administrator-assigned configurable permission sets inherited automatically at login; **no UI Switch Role section or role toggle** (Product Owner design correction, 17 Oct 2026). Every user still requires backend permission, eligible approval step and effective Company/Project scope. A multi-role user sees only the combined authorized workspace without changing identity. `projects.access_all` does not grant business approval. Technical `SYS_ADMIN` has no implicit PO/Finance approval.
8. The PDF's Nancy/Ben exceptional self-approval contradicts the ordinary maker/checker rule; **exception remains disabled pending separate approved security/business design**.
9. Three locales (`th`, `en`, `zh-TW`) and K/T/H numbering require their own proposal/configuration parity tests. Static translated examples cannot change canonical API states.
