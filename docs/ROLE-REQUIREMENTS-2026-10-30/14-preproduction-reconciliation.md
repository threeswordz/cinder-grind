Their inherited specific gaps remain attributable to the original source observations; they are not newly reproduced runtime failures. Changed Inventory, Site/Weekly and HR implementations were reconciled to their integrated slices below. The global schema has changed; this audit does not use schema equality as a blanket gap proof.

Actual Mac receipts in [#305](https://github.com/threeswordz/cinder-grind/issues/305): initial eight-role HTTP **123 checks** (12 Nov 2026); initial browser **126 checks**, eight roles plus admin (13 Nov 2026). All 112 IDs are retained. `Future gated`/`deferred` preserves existing sequencing, not an approved removal from launch scope. Each functional gap still needs governed delivery or an explicit approved launch disposition before freeze.
