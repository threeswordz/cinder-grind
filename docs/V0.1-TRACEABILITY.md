## 4. Scope independence

The V0.1 acceptance flow does not require Activities, working calendars, scheduling calculations, Gantt, site execution, BOQ/Budget, Procurement, Inventory, Subcontracts, Finance or Cost Control. Those remain in approved later releases.

## 5. Known V0.1 boundaries

- local filesystem is the prototype document provider behind a replaceable abstraction
- document links in V0.1 are Project links; WBS/Activity links are later scope
- Project actual dates are V0.2
- single-company user membership is the prototype baseline
- no offline synchronization, SSO/SCIM, multi-currency or mandatory cloud provider

These are approved scope boundaries and are not recorded as release defects unless UAT identifies an implementation problem within the approved V0.1 scope.

## 6. Human acceptance boundary

Technical green status does not complete V0.1. Staging/UAT smoke execution and Product / Business Owner sign-off remain required by the approved Testing/UAT and V0.1 Acceptance baselines.
