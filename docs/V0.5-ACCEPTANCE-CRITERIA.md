# Construction ERP — V0.5 Subcontracts Acceptance Criteria

**Status:** APPROVED by Product / Business Owner on 2026-09-29; entry-gate CI/review/merge pending  
**Entry-gate issue:** #98  
**Scope source for review:** `docs/V0.5-SCOPE.md`

AC-V05-001–030 are approved, testable interpretations of SUB-001–010 and RPT-007. The Product / Business Owner approved them with the scope and BR-V05-01–20 / D05-01–11 on 2026-09-29; implementation still waits for the entry-gate PR and Issue #98 closure.

## Subcontractor, scope and identity

- **AC-V05-001 (SUB-001):** an explicitly authorized Company user can create, read, update and archive a Company-owned Subcontractor without Project membership; code is unique within Company and historical references remain readable.
- **AC-V05-002 (SUB-001):** an inactive Subcontractor is unavailable for a new agreement, but archival never deletes approved agreement/claim history. An optional Supplier link, if supplied, must be same-Company and does not replace the Subcontracts-owned register.
- **AC-V05-003 (SUB-002/004):** an authorized Project user creates an agreement with stable Company, Project, Subcontractor, original value and Scope of Work; cross-Company and unauthorized Project references are denied by the backend.
- **AC-V05-004 (SUB-002/003/005/009):** system-assigned agreement, Work Order, claim, certification and variation numbers follow the approved sequence conventions and uniqueness scopes and remain immutable.
- **AC-V05-005 (SUB-010):** configured operational status is maintained separately from system approval state. Inactive/cancelled agreement behavior follows BR-V05-05 and never removes history.

## Agreement, Work Order and commercial ceiling

- **AC-V05-006 (SUB-002):** agreement Draft, submission, approval/rejection and revision follow the configured Approval Matrix and maker-checker restrictions; technical SYS_ADMIN cannot self-grant business authority.
- **AC-V05-007 (SUB-002/004):** the first approved original agreement value/scope remains immutable. Later non-commercial administrative revisions retain history; only approved Variations alter commercial value or Scope of Work, with actor, time, reason and historical values retained.
- **AC-V05-008 (SUB-003/004):** a Work Order references one approved agreement with its own scope/amount, optional WBS belonging to the agreement Project and optional independent Cost Code belonging to the same Company.
- **AC-V05-009 (SUB-003):** approval of Work Orders requires an approved, non-cancelled agreement and cannot allocate more than its current approved ceiling; competing approval/cancellation actions cannot bypass either guard.
- **AC-V05-010 (SUB-002/003):** agreement commercial commitment and Work Order allocation are reported without adding the same value twice.

## Claim and assessment

- **AC-V05-011 (SUB-005):** a Progress Claim identifies its agreement, claim period, positive line amounts and source Work Order where applicable, with valid same-Project references.
- **AC-V05-012 (SUB-005):** a duplicate active claim for the same agreement/period is rejected; prior rejected/withdrawn claim and replacement traceability remain visible.
- **AC-V05-013 (SUB-005):** cumulative active claimed value cannot exceed the current approved agreement ceiling, and claims against a Work Order cannot exceed its approved amount; concurrent submissions cannot evade either check.
- **AC-V05-014 (SUB-005/006):** submitted claimed amounts and later assessed amounts are separate, historically traceable values; assessment never silently changes the claim.
- **AC-V05-015 (SUB-006):** authorized assessment is nonnegative, cannot exceed the submitted claimed amount for the claim, and retains actor/time/reason and approval history.
- **AC-V05-016 (SUB-005/006):** rejected or withdrawn claims and assessed claims follow explicit correction paths; submitted/approved history cannot be edited or deleted.

## Certification and retention withholding

- **AC-V05-017 (SUB-007):** a distinct configured approver certifies a previously assessed claim through the Approval Matrix; creator/final approver separation and backend permissions are enforced.
- **AC-V05-018 (SUB-007):** certified gross value is nonnegative, at most assessed value, and cannot cause cumulative approved certification to exceed the current approved agreement ceiling.
- **AC-V05-019 (SUB-007):** one approved certification is current for a claim; its approval, amount, actor/time and source assessment are immutable, with correction through the approved replacement path.
- **AC-V05-020 (SUB-008):** retained amount is calculated from the approved agreement retention rate/base and optional cap using the approved precision/rounding; it cannot be negative or exceed certified gross.
- **AC-V05-021 (SUB-008):** cumulative retained amount respects the approved cap; net certified amount equals certified gross less withheld retention, with historical calculation inputs retained.
- **AC-V05-022 (SUB-008):** no V0.5 action releases withheld retention or posts a Finance retention ledger entry.

## Variation and reporting

- **AC-V05-023 (SUB-009):** an authorized Variation Order links to an approved, non-cancelled agreement, retains signed proposed scope/value change and enters the current agreement ceiling only after configured maker-checker approval.
- **AC-V05-024 (SUB-009):** a reducing Variation cannot lower the approved ceiling below protected approved Work Order allocation, active submitted/assessed claimed value or certified value; prior values and decisions remain immutable.
- **AC-V05-025 (RPT-007):** read-only Project-scoped reports derive original agreement, approved variation, current ceiling, approved Work Order allocation, claimed, assessed, certified, withheld retention and net certification from source records.
- **AC-V05-026 (RPT-007):** reporting separates commercial commitment from certified, Actual and Paid Cost; filters cannot reveal unauthorized Projects or cross-Company records.
- **AC-V05-027 (SUB-001–010/RPT-007):** every approved/submitted/rejected/revised action has retained audit history; concurrent action and retry cannot create duplicate approved commercial effects.

## Release quality and human exit

- **AC-V05-028:** clean migration-from-zero/status, production dependency/license audit, full V0.1–V0.5 regression, PostgreSQL integrity/concurrency/authorization tests, API/web builds and exact-head CI pass with no mandatory paid runtime.
- **AC-V05-029:** live authenticated HTTP acceptance covers register → agreement → Work Order → claim → assessment → certification/withholding → variation → reporting, plus maker-checker and unauthorized Project denial on unique scenario data.
- **AC-V05-030:** Product / Business Owner reviews the separate V0.5 business walkthrough, documents blocking defects/workarounds and explicitly accepts V0.5 before its Release Exit Gate completes or V0.6 starts.
