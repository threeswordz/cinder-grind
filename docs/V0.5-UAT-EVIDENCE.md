## Human walkthrough

Use a disposable or approved UAT Company/Project and business-role accounts. Record any blocking defect and workaround below. Do not use technical SYS_ADMIN as a substitute for business approval authority.

### 1. Subcontractor register and Agreement identity

1. Create a Company-owned Subcontractor and optionally link a same-Company Supplier.
2. Create an Agreement for an authorized Project with original value, Scope of Work, currency, retention rate/cap and operational status.
3. Confirm the Agreement receives `SCYYMM-###`.
4. Submit it through the configured Approval Matrix.
5. Verify the creator cannot final-approve their own Agreement and a configured checker can approve it.
6. Verify the first approved original value and Scope of Work remain visible as the original commercial baseline.
7. Archive the Subcontractor only after the retained history checks; confirm existing Agreement history remains readable and a new Agreement cannot use the archived Subcontractor.

**Expected:** stable Company/Project/Subcontractor identity, maker-checker, retained history and no deletion of commercial records.

### 2. Administrative Agreement revision

1. On the approved Agreement, create an administrative revision using an allowed operational status and a reason.
2. Submit and approve the revision.
3. Confirm prior Agreement versions remain readable.
4. Confirm original value, Scope of Work and currency did not change.

**Expected:** administrative revision history changes only approved non-commercial fields. Commercial changes are not folded into Agreement revisions.

### 3. Work Order allocation

1. Create a Work Order under the approved Agreement with its own scope and amount; optionally use a same-Project WBS and same-Company Cost Code.
2. Submit and approve it.
3. Verify `WO-###` is sequential within the Agreement.
4. Attempt an allocation that would exceed the current authorized Agreement ceiling.

**Expected:** valid Work Order approves through maker-checker; over-ceiling allocation is rejected and retained for an explicit decision rather than silently changing the Agreement.

### 4. Progress Claim and Assessment

1. Create a Claim with a valid period and positive line amount, linking the approved Work Order where applicable.
2. Submit it and confirm `SCLYYMM-###`.
3. Confirm the submitted claimed amount becomes immutable.
4. Assess the Claim at an amount less than or equal to the claimed amount, with reason and authorized assessor.
5. Confirm Claimed and Assessed remain separate values.
6. Exercise the approved correction path for a rejected/withdrawn/assessed Claim where appropriate and verify predecessor/replacement history remains visible.

**Expected:** duplicate active periods and ceiling/Work Order over-claims are blocked; Claim and Assessment history is retained.

### 5. Certification and retention withholding

1. Create a Certification from an assessed Claim and confirm `SCTYYMM-###`.
2. Submit it through the configured Certification workflow.
3. Verify the Certification creator cannot final-approve it even if that creator holds the approver role.
4. Approve it as a distinct configured checker.
5. Verify Certified Gross does not exceed the retained Assessment or current Agreement ceiling.
6. Verify withheld retention uses the approved Agreement rate/cap and the displayed Net Certification equals Certified Gross minus withheld retention.
7. If testing correction, reverse the Certification with a reason and verify the original snapshots remain readable.

**Expected:** Claimed, Assessed, Certified Gross, Withheld Retention and Net Certification are distinct retained values. No retention release, invoice, payment or Finance posting is created.

### 6. Variation Order

1. Create a positive Variation against the approved, non-cancelled Agreement with signed value delta, Scope change and business reason.
2. Confirm `SVOYYMM-###` and Agreement currency inheritance.
3. Retry creation with the same stable key/payload if the UAT client supports it; verify no duplicate Variation.
4. Submit the Variation through the configured Approval Matrix.
5. Verify the creator cannot final-approve it; approve as the configured checker.
6. Confirm the Agreement original value/scope remain unchanged while Current Authorized Ceiling becomes original approved value plus approved non-reversed Variation deltas.
7. Create/approve downstream allocation above the original value but within the new ceiling.
8. Attempt a reducing Variation that would take the ceiling below active approved Work Order allocation, active submitted/assessed Claim value or non-reversed certified value.
9. Attempt reversal of a positive Variation where that reversal would create the same protected-ceiling breach.

**Expected:** the safe positive Variation affects the ceiling only after approval; unsafe reduction/reversal is rejected atomically; prior Variation decisions remain retained.

### 7. Source-derived Subcontracts report

1. Open the Project-scoped Subcontracts report.
2. For the test Agreement, reconcile:
   - Original Agreement Value
   - Approved Variation Delta
   - Current Authorized Ceiling
   - Approved Work Order Allocation
   - Active Claimed Value
   - Assessed Value
   - Non-reversed Certified Gross
   - Withheld Retention
   - Net Certification
3. Verify Work Orders are not added a second time to commercial commitment.
4. Verify the report does not label commitment as Actual Cost or Paid Cost and exposes no payment/Finance posting field.

**Expected:** every amount is read-only and source-derived; there is no editable reporting/financial ledger.

### 8. Authorization and leakage checks

1. Use a user with no assignment to the test Project but otherwise relevant Subcontracts permissions.
2. Verify direct Variation detail and an explicit Project report filter are denied.
3. Verify an unfiltered report does not reveal the unauthorized Project through rows or totals.
4. Verify technical `SYS_ADMIN` role alone does not grant Variation/report business authority.
5. Verify `projects.access_all` remains a Project-scope capability and does not replace required Subcontracts action permissions.

**Expected:** backend Company/Project scope and explicit business permissions are both enforced.
