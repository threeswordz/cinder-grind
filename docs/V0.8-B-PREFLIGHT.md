- `management.dashboard.view` plus effective Project access is mandatory;
- Company isolation remains mandatory;
- protected source rows/details remain source-permission gated;
- `projects.access_all` and SYS_ADMIN do not imply Management permission;
- source mutation remains in the owning modules;
- no duplicate scheduler, procurement-risk engine, cost/finance calculation or editable KPI ledger;
- no frontend recomputation of authoritative financial Decimal values.
