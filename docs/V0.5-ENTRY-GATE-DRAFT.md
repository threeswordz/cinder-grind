These outcomes are **proposals** to review against the approved requirements. They do not fix omitted policy by inference.

## 3. Proposed acceptance areas (not numbered or approved)

- Authorized users manage active Subcontractors; inactive and cross-Company references cannot create new transactions while retained history remains visible to authorized users.
- Agreement, Scope of Work and Work Order actions enforce Company/Project scope, valid references, configured authority, traceability and retained approved history under the selected lifecycle rules.
- Claimed, assessed and certified values remain distinct, with source linkage and the chosen cumulative/partial/correction rules enforced.
- Approved certifications retain actor, time, underlying claim/assessment and withholding evidence. Withheld retention uses only the approved rate/base/rounding/cap rule. No retention release is inferred.
- Approved Variations retain scope and commercial history, with effects applied only under the chosen effective-date/value rule.
- Reports are backend authorized, read-only and derived from the source records; they do not silently post Finance, Actual Cost or Paid Cost.
- Clean migrations, API/web builds, PostgreSQL integrity and concurrency regression, authorization, audit, source-history tests, dependency/license audit, live HTTP acceptance and prior-release regression pass at the exact head.
- Product / Business Owner conducts the separate V0.5 UAT and explicitly accepts the release; automation cannot self-approve its exit gate.

Exact pass/fail examples and final AC-V05 IDs require an approved business-rule baseline.

## 4. Proposed technical baseline for review

- **Data:** existing ERD Section 13 identifies `subcontractors`, `subcontracts`, `subcontract_work_orders`, `subcontract_claims`, `subcontract_claim_items`, `subcontract_certifications` and `subcontract_variations`. Review their identity, references, immutable states, same-Company/Project integrity and approval/audit fields before any migration. No schema is approved here.
- **API/UI:** a permission-gated Subcontracts workspace with Company-scoped register maintenance for authorized Company users; agreement, Work Order, claim, assessment, certification, variation and derived reporting resources enforce Project scope. Exact routes/actions/payloads await lifecycle approval.
- **Controls:** explicit Company authorization for the Subcontractor Register; backend Project membership scope for Project-linked transactions, explicit permissions, configured Approval Matrix maker-checker where required, technical SYS_ADMIN separation, auditable history, transaction safety and no duplicate approved effects.
- **Test environment:** clean isolated regression database plus unique-scenario, non-destructive persistent-UAT runtime acceptance under existing BR-V04-20 isolation principles where applicable. Reuse current CI and open-source/zero-cost-first runtime; review any dependency change before adoption.
- **Cost boundary:** a Subcontract commitment source may later feed V0.7 Committed Cost, but V0.5 does not create a second Committed Cost ledger or classify it as Actual/Paid Cost. V0.6 owns payments and retention accounting.

## 6. Explicit exclusions and release gates

- Retention **release** rules; only withholding is in the V0.5 roadmap, subject to approved calculation rules.
- Finance payment/allocation, retention accounting, Supplier/Client Invoice, tax/VAT, FX/multi-currency and accounting journal behavior (V0.6 or later; no earlier implementation without Change Control).
- Actual Cost, Paid Cost and Cost Control ledgers or recognition policy (V0.7 and later, with V0.6 policy prerequisite). Committed, Actual and Paid Cost remain separate concepts.
- SUB-011 Finance Payment Reference and DOC-009 Subcontract/Finance Documents target V0.6; neither is pulled into V0.5 by this draft.
- Mandatory paid runtime/service and unapproved business policy are prohibited under DEC-008 and Project Governance.

**Entry gate remains OPEN.** The Product / Business Owner must explicitly approve a V0.5 scope and exclusions, numbered acceptance criteria, business rules, data/API/test and dependency baselines, build stages and any explicit deferrals in a later approval record. No V0.5-A implementation begins before that approval, exact-head CI/review and the approved entry-gate PR merge.
