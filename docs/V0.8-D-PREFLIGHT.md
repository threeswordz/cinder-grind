## Security / architecture boundaries

- Management remains read-only and does not mutate canonical source modules.
- effective Company and Project scope remains mandatory;
- explicit Management permission remains independent from `projects.access_all` and SYS_ADMIN;
- protected drilldown requires the owning module's permission;
- server/database Decimal remains authoritative;
- initial financial measures remain Company base-currency-only;
- no duplicate scheduler, procurement-risk engine, cost ledger, finance ledger or editable management KPI source;
- no unapproved inventory valuation;
- no synthetic allocation, FX conversion, predictive/ML/AI decisioning or warehouse/OLAP scope;
- V0.8-E report/export implementation remains out of Stage D;
- DEC-008 open-source / zero-cost-first remains mandatory.
