# V0.6 Finance — Acceptance Criteria Baseline

**Status:** APPROVED BY PRODUCT / BUSINESS OWNER — ENTRY-GATE TECHNICAL CLOSURE PENDING  
**Release:** V0.6 Finance  
**Issue:** #115

These criteria define the Product / Business Owner-approved V0.6 release baseline under DEC-017. They still authorize no implementation until the remaining entry-gate exact-head CI/review, merge, post-merge `main` CI and Issue #115 closure gates complete.

## Supplier Invoice / Procure-to-Pay

- **AC-V06-001** — Authorized users can create a Supplier Invoice with stable internal identity, immutable business number, valid same-Company Supplier, dates and line items.
- **AC-V06-002** — Supplier Invoice lines enforce valid Company/Project references and optional same-Project WBS / same-Company Cost Code.
- **AC-V06-003** — Where PO/GR references are used, they resolve to valid traceable Procurement/Inventory sources for the same Company/Supplier/Project context; invalid cross-scope references are rejected.
- **AC-V06-004** — Supplier Invoice submit/approve/reject uses the configured Approval Matrix, explicit permissions and maker-checker.
- **AC-V06-005** — Submitted/approved/rejected Supplier Invoice source history is retained; ordinary application flows cannot physically delete or silently rewrite approved history.
- **AC-V06-006** — Supplier/Client Invoice header totals equal the sum of retained line amounts; invalid mismatches are rejected before approval.
- **AC-V06-006A** — Procurement users can trace approved PO/GR sources forward to Supplier Invoice/Payment information where authorized, satisfying PROC-020 without transferring source ownership.
- **AC-V06-006B** — **Pending D06-17 owner decision:** under the recommended initial V0.6 rule, one Supplier Invoice belongs to exactly one Project; every line and linked PO/GR source is same-Project, and Supplier Payment/AP/cash-flow attribution inherits that Project. Cross-Project Supplier Invoices are rejected unless an alternative line-level settlement/proration and visibility model is explicitly approved.

## Client Invoice / AR

- **AC-V06-007** — Authorized users can create a Client Invoice for a valid Project + same-Company Customer with stable identity and line items.
- **AC-V06-008** — Client Invoice submit/approve/reject uses configured Approval Matrix, explicit permissions and maker-checker.
- **AC-V06-009** — Approved Client Invoice history is retained; no detailed progress-billing logic is inferred beyond the approved V0.6 baseline.
- **AC-V06-010** — AR outstanding balance is derived from approved Client Invoices and valid approved inbound allocations rather than a manually editable balance.

## AP

- **AC-V06-011** — AP outstanding balance is derived from approved Supplier Invoices and valid approved outbound allocations rather than a manually editable balance.
- **AC-V06-012** — AP/AR views apply Company and effective Project/data-scope authorization and do not become a visibility bypass.

## Payments / allocations

- **AC-V06-013** — Authorized users can create OUTBOUND and INBOUND Payments with stable identity, valid direction/counterparty, amount/date and retained audit metadata.
- **AC-V06-014** — Payment submit/approve/reject uses configured Approval Matrix, explicit permission and maker-checker; the maker cannot approve the same Payment.
- **AC-V06-015** — Only approved Payments affect settlement reporting or future Paid Cost source evidence.
- **AC-V06-016** — OUTBOUND Payments may allocate only to allowed Supplier Invoice / certified Subcontract targets; INBOUND Payments may allocate only to allowed Client Invoice targets.
- **AC-V06-017** — Cumulative allocations cannot exceed the approved Payment amount or target outstanding balance and cannot mix incompatible Company/counterparty/direction/base-currency context.
- **AC-V06-018** — Payment + allocation writes are atomic; a failed operation leaves no partial settlement effect.
- **AC-V06-019** — Duplicate/retried sensitive requests cannot create duplicate Payments or duplicate allocation effects.
- **AC-V06-020** — Concurrent allocations, approvals and cancellations cannot over-allocate, double-settle or expose stale committed state.
- **AC-V06-021** — Approved Payment correction/cancellation follows the explicitly approved BR-V06 rule and retains immutable historical evidence rather than deleting the transaction.

## Subcontracts / retention

- **AC-V06-022** — Finance can allocate an approved outbound Payment to an authorized V0.5 certified Subcontract amount and retain the certification reference.
- **AC-V06-022A** — Under approved D06-14, the cumulative active allocation ceiling for a Subcontract Certification is its V0.5 `net_certified_amount`; withheld retention is not payable through the ordinary certification allocation path.
- **AC-V06-022B** — Under approved D06-15, a Subcontract Certification whose inherited currency differs from Company base currency cannot enter Finance allocation/retention accounting; the system rejects/defer the handoff without conversion or numeric reinterpretation.
- **AC-V06-022C** — Under approved D06-16, certification reversal is blocked while active Finance Payment allocations remain; after allocations are cleared through Finance cancellation, the reversal preserves history and creates any required linked compensating retention-withholding correction rather than a retention release/payout.
- **AC-V06-023** — Subcontracts can display Finance-owned Payment status/reference for certified amounts without duplicating or editing Finance Payment data, satisfying SUB-011.
- **AC-V06-024** — Finance can derive payable retention withholding/balance information from V0.5 certification retention evidence under the approved V0.6 retention boundary.
- **AC-V06-025** — No retention release/adjustment transaction is executed unless a separate approved rule authorizes it.

## Documents / reporting

- **AC-V06-026** — DOC-009 supported Finance/Subcontract targets reuse the canonical Documents metadata/storage abstraction and enforce Company/Project/target authorization.
- **AC-V06-027** — Finance reports provide basic AP, AR, Payment, retention and Project cash-flow visibility from source records, satisfying RPT-008.
- **AC-V06-028** — Project cash flow is a derived read model from approved Finance transactions/project-attributable allocations and does not create a second editable financial ledger; unallocated Payment amounts are not attributed to a Project.

## Cost-state / accounting boundary

- **AC-V06-029** — Committed Cost, Actual Cost and Paid Cost remain separate concepts and V0.6 creates no V0.7 Cost Control ledger.
- **AC-V06-030** — COST-014 is satisfied by a Product / Business Owner-reviewed cost-recognition policy before V0.6 release exit / before V0.7 relies on Actual Cost; Finance implementation never silently invents accounting recognition.
- **AC-V06-031** — Tax/VAT, FX/multi-currency accounting, GL/journals, matching tolerances, detailed client progress billing, and Supplier/Client credit/debit-note or post-approval invoice-cancellation workflows remain excluded unless explicitly approved through Change Control.

## Security / quality / release gates

- **AC-V06-032** — Technical SYS_ADMIN authority does not imply Supplier Invoice, Client Invoice or Payment business approval authority.
- **AC-V06-033** — Backend authorization independently enforces Company/Project scope and explicit Finance permissions even if frontend controls are bypassed.
- **AC-V06-034** — Material Finance lifecycle actions and changes are auditable; ordinary users cannot edit audit history.
- **AC-V06-035** — Clean migration-from-zero, migration status, schema validation, full regression, dependency audit, API/web build/typecheck, targeted PostgreSQL integrity/concurrency tests and live HTTP Finance acceptance pass at the exact release head.
- **AC-V06-036** — No mandatory paid runtime/service or unapproved dependency is introduced; DEC-008 remains satisfied.
- **AC-V06-037** — V0.6 Product / Business Owner UAT verifies the end-to-end Supplier Invoice → approval → Payment → allocation flow, Client Invoice/receipt visibility, Subcontract payment reference, retention visibility and security boundaries.
- **AC-V06-038** — V0.6 Release Exit requires explicit Product / Business Owner acceptance. Automated tests/walkthroughs provide evidence but must not self-approve the human gate.
