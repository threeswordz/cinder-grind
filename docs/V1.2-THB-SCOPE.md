## 1. Approved boundary

All new economic monetary business operations in the existing ERP use THB. Reuse canonical sources and existing permissions, Project scope, state machines, maker/checker, numbering, Decimal calculations, idempotency and audit. No ordinary administrator currency override.

In scope:

1. Company/bootstrap defaults, existing Company Settings and new monetary paths in Project contracts, working BOQ/Budget, supplier sourcing/PO, Subcontracts, Finance/retention, Direct Cost/Project Variation/Forecast and their reports.
2. Server-owned THB provenance for new monetary roots or equivalent immutable verified parent contracts; API validation and appropriate database enforcement through create/edit/submit/approve/revision/handoff/retry paths. Remove editable new-business currency choices while displaying retained history truthfully.
3. Preserve #248's existing PO/GR-to-Supplier-Invoice currency guard and #249's limited UI-default fixes. Do not reimplement these resolved changes or claim they already delivered full THB-only operation.
4. Apply the policy to existing inputs/exports/integrations where present. Do not build new imports or modules merely to claim coverage. Future integrations must follow the same contract.
5. Minimum additive provenance/default/schema changes, forward-only migrations and a bounded schema-bearing upgrade/recovery rehearsal. No unnecessary dependency, service or architecture.
6. Existing precision and financial recognition semantics: THB does not force all rates/quantities to two decimal places. Preserve existing BOQ/quotation/settlement precision and reconciliation; Committed, Actual and Paid remain distinct.

Excluded: Internal/Customer BOQ redesign, Detailed/Lump Sum/Hybrid/UOM enhancements beyond currency provenance, new role grants/approval actors, payroll/HR calculations, multi-Project PO/Invoice/Payment, FX/GL/tax, new paid infrastructure, general migration framework expansion and Production deployment. #245 remains deferred. BOQ/HR questions in the [requirements intake](POST-V1.1-ERP-REQUIREMENTS-CONSOLIDATION-2026-10-25.md) are not bundled implementation approval.

## 2. Approved policies

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| D12-01 | **New economic business activity is THB-only.** New Companies/bootstrap use THB; no new non-THB operating currency through API, UI or direct database paths. Existing Company rows are not bulk rewritten. New monetary business writes in a non-THB Company remain blocked until its existing audited configuration path is deliberately set to THB. Nonmonetary/history access retains existing permissions. |
| D12-02 | **Historical exact corrections retain original denomination.** Only already-supported source-linked reversals/compensations may neutralize historical records under existing permission, scope, maker/checker, reason, amount and idempotency checks. They cannot introduce new foreign procurement/settlement or increased exposure. Ordinary audit/version/replay evidence preserves its original source identity. |
| D12-03 | **Foreign/unknown chains cannot acquire new commercial value.** Preserve their labels, amounts, states, stock, approvals and read access. Block unsafe new creation, revision, approval or handoff rather than relabel, convert or silently copy numbers to THB. Where the existing single-root Budget model has no safe THB replacement, leave the new monetary operation blocked until a separately approved resolution exists. New roots require immutable THB provenance or a verified parent contract. Do not infer old currency from current Company settings or dates; no automatic legacy backfill. |
| D12-04 | **Prove necessary schema-bearing upgrades and recovery.** Authorize only the additive provenance/default/schema changes needed for this currency boundary and a reviewed rehearsal that validates the real previous-release upgrade, paired database/Documents recovery, isolated restore and compatible restored runtime. Preserve application-only and SQL-only rehearsal modes. No skipped gate or application-only rollback claim across an incompatible schema. |

No grandfathering by client-provided date, Company-name prefix, mutable override or administrator privilege. Eligibility must be database-verifiable. An exact retry returning an already persisted result must retain its identity without a new business write; changed-payload replay fails. No executed-migration rewrite, Original Budget rewrite, historical currency relabel, fabricated FX, data deletion/archive cleanup or approval-history mutation.

Define minimum new-root/parent provenance; block unverifiable old handoffs and misleading aggregate currency labels. Do not infer provenance merely from absence of another currency field. |

| Release assurance | `deploy/release-candidate/rehearse.sh` rejects schema changes in application-only mode; `rehearse-migration.sh` also currently requires unchanged Prisma schema. Nullable fields alone do not establish THB provenance for new business. Unknown implementation details are technical design work within this boundary; a materially new business policy still requires Change Control.
