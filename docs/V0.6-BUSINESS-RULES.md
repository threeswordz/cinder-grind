**Status:** APPROVED BY PRODUCT / BUSINESS OWNER — ENTRY-GATE TECHNICAL CLOSURE PENDING

This document records the Product / Business Owner-approved V0.6 Finance business-rule baseline under DEC-017. Development/automation must not reinterpret or expand the approved D06 decisions without Change Control.

## 1. Approved baseline business rules

### BR-V06-01 — Finance ownership

Finance owns Supplier Invoices, Client Invoices, Payments and Payment Allocations. Procurement, Inventory and Subcontracts retain ownership of their source transactions. Finance references those sources; it does not copy them into a second source ledger.

### BR-V06-02 — Base currency / no FX

For the prototype, Finance monetary values use the owning Company's configured base currency. V0.6 performs no FX conversion, foreign-exchange gain/loss accounting or multi-currency accounting.

### BR-V06-03 — Approval framework

Supplier Invoices, Client Invoices and Payments use the existing configurable Approval Matrix. Final approval requires the explicit `*.approve` permission, authorized workflow step, correct state/scope and maker-checker.

### BR-V06-04 — Technical administrator separation

Technical `SYS_ADMIN` does not implicitly gain Finance business approval authority.

### BR-V06-05 — Supplier Invoice source linkage

A Supplier Invoice may reference valid PO and Goods Receipt source items. References must preserve Company/Supplier/Project integrity. No matching tolerance or auto-match assumption is introduced unless D06-03 is explicitly changed.

### BR-V06-06 — Supplier Invoice history

Draft commercial fields may be edited only in allowed Draft state. Submitted/approved/rejected history remains reviewable. Approved history is never silently mutated or physically deleted. Any post-approval invoice correction/cancellation mechanism is excluded from the initial V0.6 baseline unless separately approved through D06-13 / Change Control.

### BR-V06-07 — Client Invoice boundary

V0.6 supports generic Project/Customer Client Invoice lines. Detailed construction progress-billing methodology is outside baseline unless explicitly approved through Change Control.

### BR-V06-08 — Payment direction and counterparty

`OUTBOUND` Payments settle Supplier/Subcontract liabilities; `INBOUND` Payments settle Client receivables. Direction-specific counterparty and allocation validation is enforced.

### BR-V06-09 — Allocation ceilings

Cumulative allocations may not exceed the approved Payment amount or the target's outstanding approved balance. Allocation is settlement evidence; it must be atomic, auditable and concurrency-safe.

### BR-V06-10 — Derived AP/AR

AP and AR balances are derived from approved invoices and valid approved/cancel-safe allocations. There is no manually editable AP/AR balance ledger.

### BR-V06-11 — Subcontract payment reference

Finance owns Payment data. Subcontracts exposes read-only Finance status/reference for certified amounts through authorized cross-module reads, satisfying SUB-011 without data duplication.

### BR-V06-12 — Retention accounting boundary

V0.6 records/derives payable retention withholding from V0.5 certification evidence. Retention release/adjustment authorization remains deferred unless explicitly approved.

### BR-V06-13 — Derived Project Cash Flow

Project cash flow is derived from approved Finance transactions and project-attributable allocations. An unallocated Payment is not attributed to a Project until a valid source/allocation supplies Project context. It does not create an editable cash-flow ledger.

### BR-V06-14 — Cost-state separation

Committed Cost, Actual Cost and Paid Cost are separate. V0.6 owns Finance source evidence only; V0.7 owns integrated Cost Control views/ledgers.

### BR-V06-15 — Documents

DOC-009 reuses the existing Documents owner/storage abstraction. Finance/Subcontract file bytes remain outside PostgreSQL under the existing replaceable storage provider.

### BR-V06-16 — Audit and history

Material Finance actions record actor/action/time and retain decision history. Ordinary users cannot edit audit history.

### BR-V06-17 — Idempotency / duplicate-effect protection

Sensitive Payment/create/approval/allocation operations require durable duplicate-effect protection using existing approved transaction-specific keys/constraints or an approved reusable mechanism.

### BR-V06-18 — Concurrency

Payment/allocation/approval/cancellation operations lock or otherwise serialize the canonical competing resource and recheck committed state before creating settlement effects.

### BR-V06-19 — No unapproved accounting expansion

V0.6 does not introduce tax/VAT calculation, FX, GL/journals, accrual engine, chart of accounts or statutory accounting postings without Change Control.

### BR-V06-20 — DEC-008

V0.6 must run with the existing open-source / zero-cost-first stack. New mandatory paid services/components are prohibited without explicit owner approval.

## 2. Product / Business Owner decisions

The following decisions are the genuine owner choices identified from the current repository baselines.

| ID | Decision requiring explicit owner approval | Proposed choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **D06-01** | Finance business numbering for Supplier Invoice, Client Invoice and Payment. | Use existing configurable Number Sequence infrastructure with Company-scoped immutable numbers: `SIYYMM-###`, `CIYYMM-###`, `PAYYYMM-###`, MONTHLY reset. Supplier-provided invoice number remains separate `supplier_reference` and unique per Company+Supplier. |
| **D06-02** | Currency operating rule for V0.6. | Continue the Architecture/ERD prototype rule: all V0.6 Finance values use Company `base_currency_code`; no FX or multi-currency accounting. Any Finance handoff from an existing non-base-currency V0.5 subcontract source is governed by D06-15 and must never silently reinterpret the numeric amount as base currency. |
| **D06-03** | Supplier Invoice PO/GR matching policy and tolerances. | Implement source-reference integrity only; do **not** auto-approve/auto-reject based on quantity/value tolerances. Matching tolerances remain deferred Change Control. Approval users review exceptions manually. |
| **D06-04** | Client Invoice billing methodology. | Generic Project/Customer invoice lines only. Detailed progress/milestone billing is deferred Change Control. |
| **D06-05** | Payment allocation behavior. | Allow partial and multi-target allocations while cumulative allocations stay within Payment amount and each target outstanding balance. A Payment may remain partially unallocated. One Payment may allocate only compatible Company/direction/counterparty context. For Subcontract Certifications, the payable ceiling basis is governed explicitly by D06-14. |
| **D06-06** | Approved Payment correction/cancellation. | No direct edit/delete after approval. A controlled cancel action records actor/time/reason, invalidates settlement effect for derived balances and preserves the original Payment/allocation history. Re-issue uses a new Payment. |
| **D06-07** | Retention accounting extent in V0.6. | Implement payable retention withholding/balance entries from approved V0.5 certifications only. Retention RELEASE/ADJUSTMENT transaction actions and receivable retention are deferred until rules/client-billing policy are approved. |
| **D06-08** | COST-014 recognition policy used later by V0.7. | Proposed ERP cost-state policy: final approved Supplier Invoice line amount becomes eligible Actual Cost; approved Subcontract Certification `certified_amount` (gross before retention withholding) becomes eligible Actual Cost; Goods Receipt alone is not Actual Cost; payment timing affects Paid Cost only; Direct Cost Posting remains V0.7. This is an internal project-cost recognition rule, not a statutory GL/tax policy. |
| **D06-09** | DOC-009 target set. | Enable documents for Supplier Invoice, Client Invoice, Payment, Subcontract Agreement, Work Order, Claim, Certification and Variation using the existing Documents abstraction/authorization model. |
| **D06-10** | Tax/VAT handling in V0.6. | No tax engine, tax-code breakup or statutory reporting. Finance stores the approved entered base-currency transaction/line amounts only. Any future tax/VAT policy requires Change Control. |
| **D06-11** | AP/AR and Project Cash Flow source states. | Include only finally approved invoices/payments and valid non-cancelled settlement allocations in operational balances/cash flow. Draft/submitted/rejected/cancelled source transactions do not contribute. |
| **D06-12** | Release sequencing / policy timing. | D06-01 through D06-11 are approved as the V0.6 baseline before V0.6-A begins. If D06-08 is not approved now, V0.6-A may start only if the owner explicitly approves a recorded deferral that blocks all Actual Cost derivation and blocks V0.6 Release Exit until COST-014 is resolved. |
| **D06-13** | Supplier/Client Invoice correction after final approval. | Do not silently add credit/debit-note or invoice-cancellation functionality. Keep approved invoices immutable in the initial V0.6 baseline; if post-approval correction is required for release usability, treat the specific cancellation/credit-note model and any new permission as explicit Change Control before implementation. |
| **D06-14** | Subcontract Certification payment-allocation amount basis. | **APPROVED — Product / Business Owner, 2026-10-01:** Finance may allocate only up to the certification's V0.5 `net_certified_amount` (certified gross less withheld retention), less prior active allocations. Withheld retention is not payable through the ordinary certification allocation path and cannot be paid until a separately approved retention-release rule exists. |
| **D06-15** | Finance handoff for a V0.5 Subcontract Certification whose inherited currency differs from Company base currency. | **APPROVED — Product / Business Owner, 2026-10-01:** reject/defer Finance allocation and retention-accounting handoff for that certification with a clear unsupported-currency state. Do not convert and do not reinterpret the amount as base currency. FX/multi-currency support requires Change Control. |
| **D06-16** | V0.5 Certification reversal after Finance handoff. | **APPROVED — Product / Business Owner, 2026-10-01:** block certification reversal while any active/non-cancelled Finance Payment allocation remains linked. After those Payments/allocations are cancelled through Finance, certification reversal may proceed and must atomically create a linked compensating reversal of the Finance retention-withholding entry, if one exists. That system correction is not a retention release/payout; original Finance/Subcontract history remains traceable. |

## 3. What is not an owner decision because it is already governed

- backend authorization as security boundary.

- no editable AP/AR balance table and no duplicate source ledger.
- Committed/Actual/Paid Cost separation.
